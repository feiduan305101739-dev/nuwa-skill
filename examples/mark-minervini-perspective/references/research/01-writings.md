# Mark Minervini - Writings

## Core Books
1. **Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard (2011)**
   - Introduces the SEPA (Specific Entry Point Analysis) methodology.
   - Explains the Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP).
   - Emphasizes risk management as the cornerstone of superperformance.

2. **Think and Trade Like a Champion (2017)**
   - Expands on the concepts from his first book.
   - Deep dives into risk management, position sizing, and trading psychology.
   - Focuses on treating trading as a business.

## Key Concepts
### 1. Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP)
- **Definition:** A chart pattern showing progressively tighter consolidations (typically 3-4 contractions) where each pullback is smaller than the previous one (e.g., 18% -> 12% -> 6%).
- **Mechanics:**
  - Represents the transition from weak hands to strong hands.
  - Volatility compresses, volume dries up during pullbacks (supply exhaustion).
  - Breakout occurs on expanding volume (institutional demand).
- **Context:** Must happen in a Stage 2 uptrend.

### 2. SEPA Methodology
- **S**pecific **E**ntry **P**oint **A**nalysis.
- Combines fundamental analysis (earnings, sales growth) with precise technical entry points (VCP breakouts).
- Focuses exclusively on market leaders showing high relative strength.

### 3. Risk Management First
- "I want to be the last weak holder."
- Emphasizes cutting losses quickly, typically at 7-8% maximum.
- Believes that protecting capital is more important than finding winners. You can't control the market, but you can control your risk.

### 4. Trend Template
- A strict set of criteria a stock must meet before being considered for a trade.
- Price must be above the 50-day, 150-day, and 200-day moving averages.
- Moving averages must be aligned properly (50 > 150 > 200).
- The stock must be outperforming the broader market.
