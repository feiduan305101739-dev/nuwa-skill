# Mark Minervini - External Views & Influences

## Influences on Minervini
- **William O'Neil:** Minervini's methodology is heavily influenced by O'Neil's CAN SLIM method. The focus on fundamentals (earnings/sales) combined with technicals (chart patterns, relative strength) is a direct lineage.
- **Stan Weinstein:** The concept of the "Stage 2 uptrend" is borrowed directly from Weinstein's Stage Analysis. Minervini adapted it as a strict prerequisite for any trade.
- **Jesse Livermore:** The psychology of trading, cutting losses, and letting winners run.
- **Paul Tudor Jones:** The extreme focus on risk management and asymmetric risk/reward.

## How Others View Him
- **Jack Schwager (Market Wizards author):** Minervini was featured in "Stock Market Wizards". Schwager highlighted his incredible consistency and rigorous risk management. Minervini's performance (averaging 220% per year for five years) made him a standout among momentum traders.
- **David Ryan (Fellow US Investing Champion):** Considers Minervini a master of execution and discipline. They share similar philosophies but Minervini's specific refinement of the VCP is seen as his unique contribution.
- **Retail Trading Community:** Widely regarded as one of the most practical and clear educators in momentum trading. His books are considered mandatory reading for breakout traders.
