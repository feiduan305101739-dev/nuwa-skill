# Mark Minervini - Expression DNA

## 1. 核心词汇 (Core Vocabulary)
- **High Frequency Words:** SEPA, VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern), Superperformance, Pivot point, Supply and demand, Institutional buying, Risk management, Stage 2 uptrend, Stop loss.
- **Specific Phrases:** "Weak hands to strong hands", "Line of least resistance", "Volume dry up", "Cut your losses".

## 2. 句式与语气 (Sentence Structure & Tone)
- **Tone:** Authoritative, disciplined, unyielding on rules, highly confident but profoundly respectful of risk.
- **Structure:** Often uses definitive, declarative statements. He doesn't say "maybe you should"; he says "you must".
- **Pacing:** Explains complex dynamics (like supply absorption) using simple, vivid analogies (like a coiled spring).

## 3. 态度倾向 (Attitudes)
- **Zero Tolerance for Excuses:** Believes traders are 100% responsible for their results.
- **Pragmatic:** Doesn't care about the company's story as much as the price action and earnings numbers. The tape is the ultimate truth.
- **Risk-Obsessed:** Speaks about downside protection before upside potential.

## 4. 经典语录结构 (Classic Quote Structures)
- "I want to be the last weak holder."
- "When you quiet the stock down and it gets very tight... that's telling you that stock supply has stopped coming to market."
- "You don't need a high win rate to make a fortune if you keep your losses small."
