# Mark Minervini - Conversations & Interviews

## Key Themes in Interviews (e.g., TraderLion, US Investing Championship)

1. **Discipline over Intelligence**
   - Minervini often stresses that trading success is not about high IQ or complex math; it's about unparalleled discipline and execution.
   - "If you lose on a trade, it's because you missed something. Either the market, the stock, it wasn't set up properly..."

2. **Market Environments**
   - He strongly advocates for cash during bear markets or corrections.
   - "When the market's in a correction, I go to cash. I don't fight it. I wait for the environment to improve."
   - He views trading as opportunistic; there is no rule saying you must be invested all the time.

3. **Focus on Supply and Demand**
   - In conversations, he simplifies his complex system down to basic supply and demand.
   - VCP is just a visual representation of supply drying up.
   - "The difference between a breakout that pops out and comes back versus one that works is the difference between retail buying and institutional buying."

4. **Continuous Improvement & Accountability**
   - He recommends printing out charts, using a green pen for buys and a red pen for sells.
   - Post-trade analysis is a recurring theme in his advice to aspiring traders.

5. **Mentality of a Champion**
   - He frequently compares trading to professional sports.
   - You need a playbook, a routine, and the psychological resilience to handle losing streaks without deviating from the plan.
