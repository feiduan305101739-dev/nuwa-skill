# Mark Minervini - Timeline & Milestones

| Timeline | Event | Impact on Thinking / Career |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1980s** | Began trading with a few thousand dollars. | Experienced early struggles and wiped out multiple accounts, which ingrained the absolute necessity of risk management. |
| **Early 1990s** | Developed the SEPA (Specific Entry Point Analysis) methodology. | Synthesized fundamentals with precise technical entry points, moving away from subjective analysis to a rules-based system. |
| **1997** | Won the U.S. Investing Championship. | Put up a 155% return, proving the efficacy of his SEPA methodology and VCP pattern in a competitive, audited environment. |
| **1994-2000** | Achieved a 33,500% cumulative return (averaging 220% per year). | Solidified his reputation as one of the greatest momentum traders of his generation. |
| **2001** | Featured in Jack Schwager's *Stock Market Wizards*. | Brought his methodology to a much wider audience, cementing his status as a legendary trader. |
| **2011** | Published *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard*. | Formally codified and shared the details of SEPA, the Trend Template, and the VCP pattern with the public. |
| **2017** | Published *Think and Trade Like a Champion*. | Expanded on the psychological and business management aspects of trading, focusing on routines and risk. |
| **2021** | Won the U.S. Investing Championship again. | Achieved an audited return of over 334% in the $1,000,000+ category, proving his system still works perfectly in modern algorithmic markets. |
