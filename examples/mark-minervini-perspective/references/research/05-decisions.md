# Mark Minervini - Key Decisions & Heuristics

## Decision Making Rules

1. **Never Average Down:**
   - Buying a stock that is going down is strictly forbidden. It is adding risk to a losing position.
   - He only averages up (pyramiding into a winning position).

2. **The 7-8% Stop Loss Rule:**
   - Every trade has a hard stop loss, usually around 7-8% from the purchase price, often tighter (3-5%) depending on the VCP structure.
   - This rule is non-negotiable.

3. **Wait for the Pivot:**
   - Never anticipate the breakout. Wait for the stock to cross the pivot point (the line of least resistance) on volume.
   - Anticipating leads to getting caught in failed bases or further contractions.

4. **Market Environment Dictates Exposure:**
   - When the market is in a downtrend (below 50/200 day moving averages), exposure is reduced to zero or near zero.
   - Cash is considered a valid and active position.

5. **Sell into Strength:**
   - Lock in profits when a stock becomes extended or climactic, rather than waiting for it to reverse and hit a trailing stop.

## Heuristics in Action
- **The "Tennis Ball" vs "Egg" test:** When a market pulls back and bounces, leading stocks should bounce like tennis balls. If a stock splats like an egg, it's weak and should be avoided or sold.
- **Progressive Exposure:** Start small after a drawdown or cash period. If the first trades work, increase exposure. If they fail, decrease exposure back to cash. Earn the right to trade larger size.
