# External Views on Serenity

## Polarized Perceptions

Serenity is a highly polarizing figure, viewed radically differently depending on the audience.

### 1. The Reddit / WSB Moderators
- **Skeptical & Punitive**: In early 2022, after Serenity (then AleaBito) published a deep fundamental research report on $AXTI, WSB moderators permanently banned his account. They accused him of "deliberate market manipulation" and running a "pump-and-dump," as his professional rigor clashed sharply with the forum's speculative, high-leverage gambling atmosphere.

### 2. The Institutional Market & Media
- **Validation & Copytrading**: A single tweet from Serenity can send a FTSE 250 constituent stock soaring nearly 90% within two days. Hedge funds have reportedly begun copytrading his positions.
- **Media Citations**: His supply-chain research has been cited by major financial media outlets including Bloomberg and Reuters.
- **Skepticism of Background**: Markets are simultaneously awestruck by his returns (staggering 22,561.99% in two years) but skeptical of his unverifiable background claims (e.g., 'former AI research scientist,' 'author of a Nature paper,' 'member of the RISC-V Foundation,' turning down an NVIDIA AI lead offer in 2018).

### 3. Veteran Short Sellers
- **Sharp Criticism**: Veteran short sellers sharply criticize his approach, labeling it essentially a **"pump-and-dump scheme wrapped in high-IQ academic packaging."** They argue that his reliance on massive media attention to lure retail investors into highly illiquid micro-cap stocks exposes the strategy to stampede risks, persistent regulatory scrutiny, and allegations of impropriety.
